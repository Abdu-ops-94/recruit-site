01Assistant

### Talk to your database.

The assistant lives on the home screen, not in a side tab. Ask who fits a role, what a client said last week, or which roles are stuck. It answers from your data, drafts the next step, and waits for your approval before anything goes out.

02CV reading

### Every CV, read in full.

Upload a PDF, a Word file, a photo of a printed CV, or a spreadsheet of 500 people. Recruit extracts employer, seniority, function, skills, languages, education, nationality, visa, notice period and links — and spots duplicates before they become two records.

03Matching

### Scores that show their work.

Three stages: hard filters, similarity ranking, then the model reads the finalists and scores each one. Every score comes with cited evidence and the gaps. Your recruiter argues with reasons, and the client sees why.

04Client portal

### Clients say yes or no. Logged.

A client gets a signed link, sees only their roles and only the candidates you chose to send. Feedback is dated by when the client said it. No more "did they see the shortlist?" — you know.

05Privacy shield

### Contact details never leave the building.

Emails, phone numbers, addresses and links are lifted out of every CV before the model is called and put back after. Each agency's data is isolated at the database level. Deleting a candidate deletes the files, not just the row.

__08 / Questions

## Still deciding?

Who is Recruit for?__

Recruitment agencies and executive search firms — permanent and contract. From a founder working alone to a group of agencies under one brand. In-house teams use it too, but the client portal, agency isolation and white label exist because agencies asked for them.

How does "shortlist in 30 seconds" actually work?__

Three stages. Hard filters (location, right to work, availability) cut the pool. Similarity ranking finds the closest CVs to the brief. Then the model reads the finalists in full and scores each one, citing the lines that earned the score and naming the gaps. The heavy work runs on a queue, so the screen never freezes.

Does candidate data go to OpenAI or Google?__

Not contact details. Emails, phone numbers, addresses and links are removed before the model is called and put back afterwards. Only the professional content of a CV is read. On the White label tier you can bring your own model keys.

Can we migrate from Recruit CRM, Bullhorn or a spreadsheet?__

Yes. Spreadsheet import (CSV and Excel) is built in and resumable. For a full migration from another ATS we map your fields and stages, import in batches, and run duplicate detection as we go.

Is it GDPR-ready?__

Data lives in Frankfurt. Every agency is isolated at the database level. Deletion removes the record, the files and the search vectors — and logs the erasure first. Consent capture on the apply form is configurable per agency.

What does white label include?__

Your logo, colours, domain and email sender across the app, the apply page and the client portal. Reseller partners get a console to create agencies, each isolated with its own admin, billed under your name.
