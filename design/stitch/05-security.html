Frequently Answered Security Questions

Everything your Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Data Protection Officer (DPO) need to sign off.

Does candidate contact information ever reach OpenAI, Anthropic, or external LLMs?

+
No. Direct contact details—including personal email addresses, private telephone numbers, physical street addresses, and social profile handles—are stripped out in volatile memory prior to any API inference payload creation. Only anonymized skills, qualifications, seniority markers, and past company names are evaluated. Contact details are re-linked locally within your sovereign PostgreSQL instance.

How does Recruit mathematically ensure Agency A cannot read Agency B's pool?

+
Recruit relies on PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies embedded directly in the relational schema. Every API request assumes an authenticated database role tied to a specific agency tenant identity. Even if an application-layer bug were to omit a where-clause filter, the database engine returns zero rows for records that belong to another agency ID.

Can our search firm bring our own OpenAI or Anthropic API credentials?

+
Yes. On our White Label and Enterprise tiers, agencies can inject their own Zero-Retention Enterprise API keys. When configured, Recruit routes all inference calls through your direct provider contracts, ensuring your enterprise legal terms govern all model evaluations.

What occurs during a GDPR Right-to-Erasure execution?

+
Deletion is permanent and atomic. When an admin executes an erasure request, Recruit drops the candidate relational row, wipes the raw PDF/Word document from Frankfurt S3 buckets, purges the 1536-dimension vector embedding from pgvector, and cleans cached reasoning logs. A cryptographic audit signature is logged without storing the erased PII.
