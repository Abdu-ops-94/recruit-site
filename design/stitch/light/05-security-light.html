Frequently Answered Security Questions

help_outline

Can Recruit staff or engineers browse our candidate database?

No. All candidate tables are encrypted at rest with customer-managed AWS KMS keys. Production databases feature zero bastion access; engineering diagnostics rely solely on synthetic anonymized test harnesses.

help_outline

Are our candidate vectors stored alongside other search firms?

No. In addition to PostgreSQL Row-Level Security, embeddings reside in tenant-isolated index partitions. Nearest-neighbor vector queries physically cannot calculate cosine similarities across differing agency tenant scopes.

help_outline

What happens when a candidate requests deletion under GDPR?

Initiating an erasure triggers our automated purging daemon: raw CV files in S3 are expunged, database records are unlinked and destroyed, and related vector indexes are re-calculated without that candidate. A signed cryptographic receipt is returned.

help_outline

Do you retain prompts when using OpenAI or Anthropic models?

We hold explicit Zero Data Retention (ZDR) enterprise business associate agreements. Model providers do not store incoming payloads, do not perform model evaluations with our traffic, and retain zero persistent logs.
