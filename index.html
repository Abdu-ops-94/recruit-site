05 / FREQUENTLY ADDRESSED QUESTIONS

Common inquiries from boutique principals

How does Recruit ensure candidate records aren't leaked to public AI models? expand_more

We employ a pre-processing Sovereign Redaction Gateway that strips all direct PII (telephones, residential addresses, emails, civil IDs) before vector indexing. Furthermore, enterprise instances utilize dedicated private LLM endpoints bound by explicit zero-data-retention agreements.

Can we migrate our existing candidate database from Bullhorn, Vincere, or Loxo? expand_more

Yes. Our white-glove engineering onboarding team handles historical SQL and document ingestion. We migrate CVs, historical client notes, placement histories, and custom tags with full semantic re-indexing. Timeline depends on volume — we will give you an estimate before you commit.

Does Recruit support executive retained search methodologies (e.g., blinded decks)? expand_more

Precisely. With one click, generate anonymized, blinded candidate dossiers with custom client reference codes, verified track record milestones, and compensation expectations ready for board-level review.
