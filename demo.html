Evaluation Protocol FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about testing Recruit with real executive data.

security

What happens to the candidate CVs we upload?

All files uploaded during a live evaluation are processed entirely in-memory on our dedicated sovereign European instance. Once your session ends, temporary sandboxes are cryptographically shredded within 15 minutes.

psychology

Is my mandate data used to train AI models?

Never. We maintain a zero-retention enterprise contract with sovereign LLM endpoints. Your proprietary client briefs, compensation sheets, and headhunting notes never become part of any public or global model dataset.

speed

Can we test integration with our existing ATS?

Yes. During the evaluation, our partner can simulate two-way sync with Bullhorn, Vincere, or an export batch from your legacy system to demonstrate zero data loss and automated field enrichment.

share

Do we really get to keep the shortlist generated?

Absolutely. You will be provided an unbranded or agency-branded PDF/Web client link with the verbatim ranking criteria, source citations, and candidate comparison matrix to use immediately with your hiring client.
